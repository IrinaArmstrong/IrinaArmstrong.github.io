Irina Abdullaeva

I work on multimodal models that watch video. At the FusionBrain Lab I lead a project on streaming video understanding: Video-LLMs that read a video as it arrives and decide when to respond.

I train and evaluate vision-language and omni-modal models, and build the benchmarks and training infrastructure around them. The thread through my work, and through my doctoral research, is long and continuous data: long videos, event sequences and streams.

Before research I spent several years building production NLP and document-understanding systems at Sber. I am now moving toward robot learning, with a focus on vision-language-action models and world models.

Research

Streaming video understanding

Video-LLMs that process a stream causally and choose when to answer. I lead this project with a team of five or more. We built a unified evaluation framework for offline and streaming Video-LLMs, covering omni-modal and proactive-response settings. SPECTRA scores capability, timing and resource use.

Efficient long-video input

MaxInfo selects key frames without training, using the maximum volume principle, and adds 3.28% on LongVideoBench with LLaVA-Video-7B. DiffSelect makes selection differentiable and query-agnostic, with Gumbel-Softmax masking and a reconstruction objective.

Building multimodal models

OmniFusion joins a pretrained LLM to visual encoders through adapters, trained in stages. ESQA connects an event-sequence encoder to a frozen LLM with a learned connector and LoRA. I also work with MiniCPM-o and Qwen-VL / Qwen3-VL.

Grounding and interpretability

LatentMask supervises latent location tokens during training to ground what a VLM sees, then discards them at inference. I also study how vision-LLMs perceive visual data.

Benchmarks

NoReGeo has 2,500 school-level geometry problems in 25 categories, built to test geometric understanding without reasoning. The best proprietary model reaches about 65%, below the 74.5% humans score on the same multiple-choice tasks. OmniDialog and MindShift cover generalization across modalities and model reactions to psychological prompts.

Long sequences

The subject of my doctoral research: methods for handling long data sequences in multimodal deep learning models, from event sequences and sensor signals to long videos.
